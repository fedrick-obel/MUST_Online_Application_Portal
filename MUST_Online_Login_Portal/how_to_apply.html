How to Apply

Follow these steps to prepare your university application.

Step 1: Create an Account

Click the Create Account button and enter your personal details to register on the portal.

Step 2: Sign In

Use your registered email address and password to access your account.

Step 3: Complete the Application Form

Enter the required personal information, academic qualifications and programme choices.

Step 4: Prepare Your Documents

Prepare the academic documents and other information required for your application category.

Step 5: Review and Submit

Check that your information is correct, then follow the official application instructions for submission and any required fees.

Important Information

Application requirements, deadlines and fees may vary. Check the official MUST website for the latest information before applying.
